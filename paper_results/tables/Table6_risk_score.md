## Table 6. Points-based risk score for therapeutic failure (derived from the multivariate models)

Integer points assigned to each dichotomised predictor. Apparent (development) performance; external validation required.


**Pre-treatment score** (range 0-8 points)

| Risk factor | Points |
|---|---|
| Male sex | 1 |
| Age <= 28 years | 2 |
| Regional lymphadenopathy | 1 |
| Concomitant infection (pre-Tx) | 2 |
| Immature granulocytes <= 0.01 x10^3/uL | 1 |
| Platelets <= 269 x10^3/uL | 1 |

Performance: AUC 0.788 (95% CI 0.713-0.862). At cut-off >= 3 points: sensitivity for failure 100%, specificity 40%, PPV 0.233, NPV 1.
