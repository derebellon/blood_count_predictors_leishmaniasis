## Table 6. Points-based risk score for therapeutic failure (derived from the multivariate models)

Points were assigned to each dichotomised predictor in proportion to its regression coefficient, following the points-system method of Sullivan et al. (the smallest coefficient defining one point). Discrimination is reported as the optimism-corrected (cross-validated) AUC; external validation in an independent cohort is still required.

**Pre-treatment score** (range 0-8 points)

| Risk factor | Points |
|---|---|
| Age <= 28 years | 2 |
| Concomitant infection (pre-Tx) | 2 |
| Male sex | 1 |
| Regional lymphadenopathy | 1 |
| Immature granulocytes <= 0.01 x10^3/uL | 1 |
| Platelets <= 269 x10^3/uL | 1 |

Performance: cross-validated AUC 0.74 (95% CI 0.70-0.76; apparent 0.79). At the development cut-off >= 3 points: sensitivity for failure 100%, specificity 40%, PPV 0.23, NPV 1.00.

**End-of-treatment score** (range 0-7 points)

| Risk factor | Points |
|---|---|
| Concomitant infection (EoTx) | 2 |
| Male sex | 1 |
| Age <= 28 years | 1 |
| Regional lymphadenopathy | 1 |
| Eosinophils >= 14% | 1 |
| Monocyte ratio (EoTx/pre) >= 1.07 | 1 |

Performance: cross-validated AUC 0.71 (95% CI 0.69-0.73; apparent 0.79). At the development cut-off >= 3 points: sensitivity for failure 71%, specificity 71%, PPV 0.33, NPV 0.93.
